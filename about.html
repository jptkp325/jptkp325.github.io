About

I build products, coach people, and breakdance.

I also ride bikes, keep trying to read more, and have a game backlog that only grows. Here's the whole picture.

What I do

Product leadership

I'm the VP, Product at Setpoint, where I lead product development for the core offering. Before that I spent five years at Policygenius, and earlier roles at Movable Ink, FanDuel, Ladders, and Nielsen, figuring out how to build the right things for the right people at the right time.

Coaching & advising

I coach product managers one-on-one on careers and interviews, and advise early-stage teams on product thinking. It's how I pay forward everything I learned the hard way.

How I got here

I didn't plan on product.

I graduated from Columbia in 2009, into the wreckage of the financial crisis, with no idea what I wanted to be. I took a spot in Nielsen's rotational program because it let me try a few different jobs, and somewhere in those rotations I found product and fell for it.

My first lesson came fast. I thought product was about defining the thing you build. It's really about understanding your customer, your market, and the business around them. Defining the product is the easy part.

The next decade was startups and growth-stage companies, starting with FanDuel and Ladders. I was the founding product hire at HealthKick, where I wrote the first version of the app myself and found product-market fit. At Movable Ink I took the product to its next stage with Studio, a design interface that lifted sales win rates by 40%. I taught aspiring PMs at Product School on the side. I did my MBA at Columbia alongside a full-time job.

At Policygenius I was the first product hire on a new insurance line and stayed five years, going from senior PM to director. We took that line from zero to a $32M revenue run rate. I also hired and managed PMs there, and that's where I got my second lesson, the harder one. I didn't know how to manage people with different backgrounds from mine. I managed everyone the way I wanted to be managed, and not everyone is like me. Everyone needs help in their own way, and learning that took longer than I'd like to admit.

Today I'm VP of Product at Setpoint, where I've taken the product from early product-market fit to a $20M revenue run rate. I coach because I believe in paying it forward. People helped me along the way, and I think it's our responsibility to pass that on. I've believed that since I was 16, helping others with SAT prep. I'm not interested in handing you a framework. I'd rather help you work out what's actually going on in your own situation.
