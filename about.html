What I do

Product leadership

I'm the VP, Product at Setpoint, where I lead product development for the core offering. Before that I spent five years at Policygenius, and earlier roles at Movable Ink, FanDuel, Ladders, and Nielsen, figuring out how to build the right things for the right people at the right time.

Coaching & advising

I coach product managers one-on-one on careers and interviews, and advise early-stage teams on product thinking. It's how I pay forward everything I learned the hard way.

How I got here

A lot of luck and some hard lessons.

In my final year at Columbia, I thought I would end up in finance. I ended up walking right into the wreckage of the financial crisis. It validated a lingering doubt that this was the best path forward but was unsure of where to go. I got lucky, I found a place in Nielsen's Emerging Leaders rotational program, which I joke, let me figure out what I wanted to be when I grew up. Somewhere, I met the people who introduced me to product.

At that point, product was about ownership and defining the thing you build. It was empowering, but big companies moved too slowly for my taste. I was learning about Scrum and Lean Startup and I wanted to get into the action. It wasn't easy, but I got lucky again and escaped from big company into the startup world.

The next decade was learning that product was really about understanding your customer, your market, and the business around them. It's where I fell in love with the craft of product management. I believe I could be the best product person by honing my product skills rather than picking an industry, which at the time was a novel concept. This led me to PM roles at FanDuel and theLadders, a founding product role at HealthKick, and a product leadership role at Movable Ink. As my career advanced, I wanted to pay it forward. On the side, I taught aspiring PMs at Product School.

Then, I made the biggest move of my career. I was the first product hire at Policygenius on their home and auto insurance product. I stayed for five years, growing the business into a profitable business line and growing myself from senior PM to director. I also hired and managed PMs there, and that's where I got my second lesson, the harder one. I learned what great looks like. I had always thought I was great, but Policygenius took my product and people management skills to the next level.

Today I'm VP of Product at Setpoint. I manage a team of PMs focused on a multi-product offering for banks and credit funds. I coach because I believe in paying it forward. People helped me along the way, and I think it's our responsibility to pass that on. I'm not interested in handing you a framework or selling you a program. I'd rather help you work out what's actually going on in your own situation.
