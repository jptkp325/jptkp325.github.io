Working With Me

I've been building products for over 15 years, and the thing I keep coming back to is this: the best PMs aren't the ones with the most frameworks or the sharpest decks. They're the ones who know themselves well enough to lead through the messy, ambiguous parts of the job.

That's what I try to help with. Not a prescriptive playbook or a one-size-fits-all system, but the kind of honest, sometimes uncomfortable thinking that actually makes you better at the work — and more confident doing it.

What It's Like

It usually starts with a conversation. You tell me what's going on — maybe you're stuck on a career decision, maybe your team dynamics are a mess, maybe you just want someone who gets it to think alongside you for a while.

From there, we figure out what makes sense together. Sometimes it's a few focused sessions on a specific challenge. Sometimes it's ongoing coaching over a few months. There's no rigid structure because your situation isn't rigid.

What I can tell you is that I take it seriously. I'll push you when you need pushing, and I'll listen when that's what matters more. I've been in the seat — leading product teams, navigating stakeholder politics, making hard calls with imperfect information. I'm not coaching from theory. I'm coaching from the same trenches you're in.

Some of the things people come to me for:

  • Career strategy — navigating your next role, going from IC to lead, or figuring out if you're even on the right path
  • Stakeholder & team dynamics — managing up, building influence without authority, getting alignment when nobody agrees
  • Product craft — sharpening your strategy, prioritization, and execution with real-world perspective, not just textbook frameworks

Let's talk.

If any of this sounds like what you need, let's start with a conversation. No pitch, no pressure — just a real talk about where you are and where you want to go.
